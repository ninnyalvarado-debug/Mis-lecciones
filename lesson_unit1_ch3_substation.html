Warm-up & Schema Activation
⏱ 20 min
🎯Objective
Activate what the student already knows before introducing formal vocabulary. The goal is no wrong answers — this is exploration, not evaluation.
📷 Activity 1A — Picture Talk (8 min)
Show the PEMEX substation photo on screen. Ask the student to describe what he sees using simple English:

"I see... / There is... / There are... / I think this is..."

Teacher writes unknown words on the Zoom whiteboard as they come up. Don't correct grammar yet — only add vocabulary.
💡 Zoom tip: open whiteboard side by side with this screen. Write the words the student attempts in English.
🗣️ Activity 1B — Prediction question (7 min)
Ask aloud: "What is the role of a substation? What happens to the electricity here?"

Student answers in English — teacher rephrases the answer correctly and writes the improved sentence on the whiteboard. Student repeats the corrected version.
📌 Session anchor — keep this on screen all class (5 min)
Tell the student: "By the end of today, if your US supervisor calls and asks: 'What is happening on site today?' — you will be able to answer in English."

Write this question on the Zoom whiteboard and leave it there the whole session.

3
Functional Grammar
⏱ 55 min
⚙️Teaching approach
Grammar is never taught in isolation. Every structure is introduced as a real communication need: "This is what you say when your US supervisor calls at 9am."
Grammar 1 — Present Continuous
Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing + object / location
Real site sentences — student drills these aloud
We are installing the conduit on the second level.
Estamos instalando la canaleta en el segundo nivel.
The crew is working on the cable trays right now.
El equipo está trabajando en las charolas ahora mismo.
The transformer is being positioned near the panel.
El transformador se está posicionando cerca del tablero.
The area is not ready yet. We are still waiting for the release.
El área no está lista todavía. Seguimos esperando la liberación.
We are not working on the grounding today — it is pending.
No estamos trabajando en la puesta a tierra hoy — está pendiente.
Grammar 2 — Passive Voice (Introduction)
Subject + was / were / has been + past participle
The engineering way to speak — focus on the result, not who did it. Very common in technical reports.
Real site sentences
The transformer was installed yesterday.
El transformador fue instalado ayer.
The supports were fabricated off-site.
Los soportes fueron fabricados fuera del sitio.
The area has not been released yet.
El área todavía no ha sido liberada.
The circuit breaker was replaced last week.
El interruptor fue reemplazado la semana pasada.
The cable tray was installed on Monday. The conduit has been connected.
La charola fue instalada el lunes. La canaleta ha sido conectada.
🔁 Activity 3A — Transform the sentence (10 min)
Teacher reads an active sentence → student converts to passive aloud. No writing needed — only speaking.

"The crew installed the conduit." → "The conduit was installed."
"They replaced the fuse." → "The fuse was replaced."
"The team has not released the area." → "The area has not been released."

Do 8–10 sentences. Focus on fluency — correct after the attempt, not during.
4
Real Communication & Speaking
⏱ 50 min
📞Role play setup
Teacher plays the US supervisor on the phone. Student plays the Mexican site coordinator. Use the PEMEX photo as reference. Do the role play 3 times — first reading, then with notes, then without notes.
Supervisor says
"Good morning! I'm calling to check on the substation project. What's happening on site today? Can you give me an update?"
Student must include (3–4 sentences)
1. What is currently in progress (Present Continuous)
2. What has already been completed (Passive Voice)
3. What is still pending
✏️Sample answer (teacher reference)
"Good morning. We are currently installing the conduit on the second level. The transformer has already been positioned near the switchboard. The cable tray is still pending — the area has not been released yet. We expect to finish by Thursday."
📋 Activity 4B — PEMEX Activities Report (20 min)
Give student a list of "Actividades por ejecutar" from the PEMEX document. For each item, student must:

1. Translate to English
2. Say it as a Present Continuous sentence (in progress)
3. Say it as a Passive Voice sentence (if completed)

Example: "Instalación de charola de cable"
→ "We are installing the cable tray."
→ "The cable tray has been installed."
💡 This activity directly uses the student's own work documents. High motivation, maximum relevance.
🎤 Activity 4C — Final spoken site report (10 min)
Student gives a 1-minute site report in English with no prompts — only the PEMEX photo.

Must include:
• What is currently happening (Present Continuous)
• What has already been completed (Passive Voice)
• What is still pending

Teacher listens without interrupting. After delivery: give feedback on vocabulary and grammar — 3 things done well, 2 things to improve.
